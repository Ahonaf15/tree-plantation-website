About Our Campaign

Why Planting Trees Matters Today More Than Ever

Deforestation and rapid urbanization have severely impacted our planet's ecosystems. Green Earth started with a simple mission: empower individuals and communities to actively participate in restoring green cover across urban and rural landscapes.

Every tree sponsored through our campaign is planted by local environmental volunteers, nurtured for its first three critical years, and geotagged so you can track the real-world ecological impact of your contribution.
